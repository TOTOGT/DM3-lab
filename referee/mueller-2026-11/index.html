Observations for the author

  1. The √2 in eq A32. vcosmo/vkinematic = √2 follows from the definitions: one uses φ−1, the other (φ−1)/2, so the ratio is √2 for any small φ−1 (tested at 10−9 and 10−4). It is not independent evidence.
  2. Eq A23, units. GM + 2R³ adds m³ s−2 to m³. It is valid once τ̂ = 1 s is inserted, as the dimensional note says. Numerically vorbit equals √(2GM/R) to about 10−31, so A23 adds nothing beyond the escape speed.
  3. Appendix A numerology. The paper discloses that α(2−α) was chosen after 2α fit less well (recomputed: 0.39% against 0.025% off). With about one choice per body, agreement near 0.5% is expected by chance. Consider setting these apart from the geometric results.
  4. Dipole comparison. 371.58 km/s against 369.82 km/s is 0.48%. The paper already notes this exceeds the stated uncertainty.
  5. §2.5.1. arccos(1/3) = 70.53°, so “below 70.5°” is slightly too tight.
  6. Prop. 1 (the derived lapse). The ODE step is correct (Lean-checked). Its status is an equivalence given the imported static vacuum equations, which §2.5 states. The abstract’s “not postulated” holds only with that qualifier kept.
