The Lorentz factor as the slope of an additional spatial coordinate: what the checks do and do not say

The paper checked here: Mueller, A. (2026). The Lorentz Factor as the Slope of an Additional Spatial Coordinate: Embedding, Painlevé–Gullstrand Flow, and the Issue Of Simultaneity (Version 1) [Preprint, 62 pp.]. Zenodo, published 16 November 2026. doi:10.5281/zenodo.24280888. The author gives his affiliation as Independent Researcher, Milwaukee, WI, USA. Read from the PDF supplied by the project owner; section and equation numbers on these pages are the paper’s.

We checked whether the paper’s displayed algebra follows from its stated premises and whether its quoted numbers recompute. Every check we ran passed; the physical inputs are imported from general relativity, as the paper itself says.

Status. Working notes, not peer reviewed, and the paper’s author has not seen them. They show that the displayed algebra and quoted numbers were rechecked, not that the paper is right or wrong.
Scope. This is not a review of the physics, and not an endorsement or refutation. Not read: §1 and §2.1 with Table 1, §2.3.6, §2.4.1–2.4.2, §4.1–4.5, Appendix A.1–A.3 and A.6, the Appendix B derivations, and the references. No rotation-curve data were available, so no χ² was recomputed. Prepared with Claude (Anthropic) assisting. Nothing here has been sent to the author. Done in the DM3-lab at no cost and for no client. A guide to how it was done, with the mistakes, is at how-to.
