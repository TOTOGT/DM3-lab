What is formalized

The target is Howe, Huffman, Kohlmeyer and Kruer, “Euler’s 1747 conjecture on sums of integers with restricted prime factors” (2611.00370v1): every n ≡ 2 (mod 4) is a sum of two positive integers whose prime factors are all ≡ 1 (mod 4). This is an independent formalization, not the authors’ own. The analytic step uses Hurwitz quaternions and Hecke operators on a finite quotient of PGL₂(𝔽ₚ), and that machinery is now in Lean.

What is not proven

H31gt3, Prop 3.1 for primes p > 3 not dividing m, is the one open hypothesis of the Euler theorem. The paper’s route needs a norm bound on Tₘ away from span{1, χ}, which rests on Jacquet–Langlands and the weight-2 Ramanujan–Petersson bound. Neither is in Mathlib.

The eigenvalue results cover the two exceptional modes only, not the rest of the spectrum. The Hecke relations that need a Hecke prime are proved for odd n only. It is not shown that χ is nonzero or different from the constant function. The final Euler theorem lists the native_decide axiom for the finite range, so it is not kernel-only.

How results are checked

Public ledger

Every numbered statement of the paper has one status word: kernel-checked, native-decide, computed by a script, read by hand, or open. Hand arguments are labelled as such.

Axiom gate in CI

Each new stage compiles on push and fails on any compile error, any sorry, a missing axiom report, or an axiom outside propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound.

Statement audit

A line-by-line audit of the paper’s sections 2 to 6, plus Python scripts that test its constants and bounds on thousands of cases. Those are labelled as tests, not proofs.
